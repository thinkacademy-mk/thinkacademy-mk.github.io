02 YOUR PERSONAL PRACTICE PLAN

Turn mistakes into next steps

A question-by-question review, with the key idea to revisit.

  1. Day 2 · Q4

    Folding & vertices

    Follow the fold and count the new corners. Folding does not always reduce the number of vertices.

  2. Day 3 · Q1

    A clock in the mirror

    Check how a reflection reverses the positions of the hands before reading the time.

  3. Day 3 · Q4

    A cube filled with sugar

    Match the volume units first: 1 m³ = 1,000 dm³. Then compare the quantities.

  4. Day 5 · Q3

    Comparing shaded areas

    Split the shaded regions into equal pieces so their areas can be compared directly.

  5. Day 6 · Q4

    Squares & equilateral triangles

    Label the 90° and 60° angles, then use the relationships around the unknown angle.

  6. Day 7 · Q3

    Counting rectangles

    Count both small rectangles and larger rectangles made by joining smaller ones.
